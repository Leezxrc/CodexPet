# 角色素材

白龙素材：`../assets/pet-v2.png`，1024 × 1536，包含透明 Alpha 通道。

圆脸 Q 版：`../assets/pet-chibi.png`，1254 × 1254，用户选定并重新附上的透明素材，直接使用该附件，没有重新绘制。

白龙与最初的圆脸角色均使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。白龙参考用户提供的三张角色设定图，以第三张的正面造型为主要依据。圆脸版本随后由用户选定，现与白龙一起提供切换；仓库只保留实际使用的两份素材。

角色保留白发、白色分节龙角、紫眼、尖耳、淡紫龙翼、白紫鳞尾、白色衣裙及结形饰品。底部文字未作为角色设计元素纳入。

以下为最终生成提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production transparent PNG standing full-body sprite for a Windows desktop companion. The user says these three reference images define the character. Image 1 and 2 establish character identity and costume. Image 3 is the PRIMARY proportion and art-style reference: match its front-facing chibi figure, approximately 3 heads tall, NOT adult realistic proportions, NOT a new redesign. Create ONE front-facing full-body character, standing relaxed, with precisely these identifying features: long flowing white hair with very pale lilac shadows, curled single ahoge, two upward white segmented dragon horns, pointed ears, soft violet eyes, reserved calm slightly sleepy expression; white and silver high-neck fantasy Chinese-inspired long gown with pale lavender lining and cascading detached sleeves; silver interwoven-knot ornaments at one side of head and waist with tiny tassels; two small pale lavender dragon wings visible at the sides, a fluffy-scaled white/lilac dragon tail curling around one side. Make the chibi outfit modest, closed and comfortable, no emphasized anatomy. Refined clean dark-lavender outlines, soft pastel anime shading; pale white/lavender palette. Keep all horns, wing tips, tail and feet fully in frame. Centered silhouette about 90% of image height with a small transparent margin. True transparent alpha background. No white backdrop, no floor, no checkerboard drawn in, no text, no signature, no UI, no watermark, no timestamp. Ignore the reference's bottom watermarks; they are not character design elements. Single front pose only, NOT multiple views or a contact sheet. Remain readable at 180-220 pixels. Preserve the provided character identity and the third reference's costume, horns, wings and tail.
```

运行时按窗口大小做高质量缩放，静止时保持原始宽高比。白龙摸头使用轻微旋转；圆脸 Q 版以可见底部为固定点，纵向压缩时按反比横向变宽，三次逐渐减弱后恢复原比例。两者都有本地爱心与短句反馈。不修改原图，不调用模型。
